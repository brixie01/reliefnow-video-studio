# ReliefNow Video Studio: editing system

The ReliefNow Laser Centers team (reliefnowlasercenters.com) edits patient-education, provider and ad videos here with Claude. Reply in the language the user writes in (English or Taglish), keep it short, and lead with results.

This is a rebrand of Joshua Ladero's open-source Video Edit Studio (MIT, see `LICENSE`). "Lloyd" in the older notes and code comments is the original creator; his editing rules still apply, the brand below replaces his.

**For any editing request, use the `reliefnow-video-edit` skill** (`.claude/skills/reliefnow-video-edit/SKILL.md`). It holds the full workflow, the `decisions.json` format, format structures, and brand rules.

## Non-negotiables
1. **Complete message.** Never drop words inside a kept sentence, never splice half-takes, and when unsure keep the passage and flag it. Missing words and choppy splices were the #1 cause of revisions before.
2. **Long-form stays long-form.** 5- and 16-minute videos are not Reels: remove only dead air, fillers, retakes, false starts. Don't trim content for length.
3. **Fast loop.** Transcribe → one thorough editorial pass → 720p preview + `verify` → deliver with a cut list → revise by timestamp → final.
4. **Brand = ReliefNow green.** Green `#8BBE29` on charcoal `#141820`, white text, Open Sans, the brand mark top-left. **Logo:** `decisions.json "logo_style"` picks it (`vedit.logo_overlay`): `"badge"` (default: both marks, ReliefNow Centers | Ditch the Drugs and Skip the Scalpel, on the black pill `assets/logo_badge.png`; on 9:16 it spans y ≈ 33–220 of 1920, ≈ 82 % of the width), `"logo"` (ReliefNow Centers only), `"tagline"` (Ditch the Drugs only) or `"none"`. Follow the user's words: "just the logo", "tagline only", "no logo" etc. set it; with nothing said, leave the badge. The progress bar sits just under whichever mark is on. Keep hooks, titles and graphics clear of the mark's strip. The official logo files (transparent PNG, original colours) are in `assets/brand/`: `reliefnow-centers-logo.png` and `ditch-the-drugs-skip-the-scalpel.png` (the tagline mark, ®). Use those for end cards / CTA scenes; `assets/logo.png` is the watermark copy with the grey turned white so it reads on footage. Never redraw or retype the logo. The templates in `mograph.py` / `mograph_more.py` are still drawn in orange `#FF6A00`: `tools/brandkit.py` recolours them to the brand accent at render, so keep writing new templates in `#FF6A00` and never hard-code the green. Health claims stay within what reliefnowlasercenters.com already says; never invent statistics, cure claims or patient results.
5. **Access.** No sign-in by design (the team chose that): the Studio listens on 127.0.0.1, or on the Tailscale IP from `studio.json` "listen" with names in "allowed_hosts" (README → Share it). `tools/editor/guard.py` refuses foreign Host / Origin and path tricks and writes `logs/activity.log`; Remove / Replace move projects to `trash/` for 30 days (`studio_api.empty_trash` on start). Never make it listen on 0.0.0.0 or put it behind a public URL.
6. **No MMS.** The literal model (`facebook/mms-1b-all`) is non-commercial, so it is off (`vedit.MMS_ON`) and not installed: `literal.py` / `wordcheck.py` skip, `splicecheck.py` shows only the energy strips, and Edit Health has no "said twice" items. Catch doubles and clipped words from the Whisper words, `tools/listen.py` windows and the energy strips instead, and listen to every join before delivering. Never download MMS. **The look of every new video follows `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`**: v5 · Newsroom (§14) for AI-update/news videos (thumbnail hook, editorial pages, real source screenshots, cam bubble); v4 · Glass (§13): glass scenes over frosted footage, text behind the speaker, big `pop` captions (110), kinetic lockups, step-by-step mock-UIs, 2–3 face breaks, Inter Tight + Great Vibes, natural footage, never cover the face.

## The Studio (step-by-step wizard) + Fine-tune editor
http://localhost:8765/ opens **the Studio** (product name editable in the UI → `studio.json`, default "ReliefNow Studio"; never hard-code a brand name in the UI) (`tools/editor/studio.html` + `studio_api.py`): Start → Colours → Fonts → Hook → Captions → Visuals → Extras → Watch, EN/Taglish toggle. Every choice is saved to `work/<name>/decisions.json` → `"look"` (palette, accent, fonts, visuals {density, types}, extras {elements, motion}, done) plus the real keys (`caption_style`, `caption_size`, `caption_y`, `captions`, `progress_bar`, `punch_in`, `mograph_theme`, `sfx`, `hook_zoom`, `keywords`); the Hook step rewrites the hook scene in scenes.json (marked `"hook": <brandkit.HOOKS id>` — 11 variants built on behind / lockup / thumb / tag; `tag` has `style` comment | counter | typing). Caption styles: pop, highlight, box, outline, punch, classic. Card previews play with the real sfx.py sounds (`/sfx/<name>.wav`). `tools/brandkit.py` applies the look at render time (mograph recolours + swaps fonts, vedit captions/progress bar use the accent + caption font). **Before editing a video, read `look`**: follow `visuals.density` (light ≈25 %, medium ≈45 %, heavy ≈70 % graphics), prefer the ticked `visuals.types`, and place the ticked "Claude places it" extras (comment pop-up, counter, typing line, script line). With no `look`, everything renders as ReliefNow Green + Open Sans/Great Vibes (`brandkit.DEFAULT_PALETTE`, `DEFAULT_FONTS`). The old editor is now **Fine-tune** at /editor:

`tools/editor/server.py` is the review editor at http://localhost:8765 (v2 UI, ReliefNow green + charcoal; v1 kept as `index_v1.html`). **Review panel**: Parts (every content cut with Claude's reason → ▶ listen to the original, ✓ Approve / ↺ Restore), Scenes (every motion graphic with hover preview → ✓ Approve / ✕ Remove = `disabled` in scenes.json), Issues (QC: doubles, possibly clipped words, joins — ↻ Run QC runs literal/wordcheck/splicecheck), Flags. **Transcript** shows every word, removed ones struck through with the reason. Approvals live in `work/<name>/review.json`. Start it with the `editor` config in `.claude/launch.json` (preview_start name "editor") or with `.venv/bin/python tools/editor/server.py`. With it he can click words to listen, select and remove/restore, fix caption text, Save (runs `vedit plan`), "Play edited", and Render preview/final (plan → mograph → render). It writes the same `work/<name>/decisions.json`; the first manual save backs up the previous one to `decisions.manual-backup.json`. If he edited manually, re-read decisions.json before changing it.

## Studio v2 (2026-10-21)
- **Install (when someone asks you to install / set up this repo):** on a Mac run `./tools/setup.sh` (or `--lite`); on Windows run `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/setup.ps1` (or `-Lite`). Then `./vedit doctor` (Windows: `./vedit` works in Git Bash, `vedit.cmd` in PowerShell / cmd), then start the Studio in the background with `./vedit studio` → http://localhost:8765. Tell them what each ✗ in `doctor` means and how to fix it (Mac: the Xcode Command Line Tools for the Swift tools, `xcode-select --install`).
- **Windows:** the Python is `.venv/Scripts/python.exe` (Mac: `.venv/bin/python`). The Apple Vision tools have Python twins (`tools/facebox.py`, `textbox.py`, `personmask.py`); always reach them through `vedit.finder(name)`, never `tools/bin/…` directly. Transcription is faster-whisper (`VEDIT_WHISPER` picks the model), H.264 is libx264 when VideoToolbox is missing (`vedit.h264()`), HDR goes through ffmpeg's zscale tone mapping (Mac keeps Apple's `tosdr`). Paths saved to JSON use `/`. `tools/ci_smoke.py` runs the whole path; the Windows GitHub workflow runs it on every push.
- **Edit Health** (`tools/health.py`, Watch step): repeats, clipped joins, dead air, HDR colour, graphics over the face (`tools/bin/facebox`), app-button safe zones, hook in the first second, loudness, lip-sync. Run it before every delivery; fix every sev-3 item.
- **Talk-to-edit**: the creator's timestamped notes live in `work/<name>/comments.json` ({at (edited s), src (source s), text, done}). When asked to "apply my notes", fix each open note, then mark it done.
- **Brief + style references** (`tools/editor/studio_brief.py`, `tools/refstyle.py`): `decisions.json` `look.brief` = {answers (platform, vibe, captions, graphics), text} is the creator's own words about the design: follow it before anything else. `look.style_refs` / `look.style_notes` are styles learned from videos they like (`refs/<id>/style.json`: caption case/position/size, highlight colour, cuts per minute, graphics share). `look.pace` (fast / medium / calm) is how tight the cuts should be. Never use a reference video's footage.
- **Auto-edit / Apply notes** run you headless (`claude -p`, `tools/editor/studio_pro.py` → `claude_run`) on this folder with Read/Edit/Write + `.venv/bin/python` / `./vedit` only. Do the full editorial pass, never render the final, never touch files outside this folder.
- **Automatic hook** (`studio_pro.auto_hook`, after every import): Hook Lab's top line becomes the cold open; a video too short for one gets a `thumb` hook. If Claude Code is installed, `claude_headline` then rewrites the title (scene marked `"ai_title": true`). It never replaces a hook that is already set; `studio.json` `"auto_hook": false` turns it off. During Auto-edit you may improve the automatic hook; keep its headline within what the video says.
- **Hook Lab** sets `decisions.json "hook": {from, to}` — a COPIED cold open (the body still runs through the line) + a `cold_open` thumb scene. `"move": true` exists but Lloyd rejected it.
- **2026 motion library** (`tools/mograph_more.py`, catalog in `reference/motion_trends_2026.md`): 43 scene types — scribble, target, ping, keys, island, search, post, call, tier, score, price, timer, ring, chart, timeline, streak, flap, glitch, redact, define, pov, rank, ransom, flip, polaroid, achieve, sticker, burst, rail, ff, plus the next wave: wipe (before/after slider, images), loupe (magnifier on a screenshot), thread (forum card), voicenote, feed (feed-scroll phone), receipt, venn, cloud (word cloud), slot (slot reels), tear (torn paper), board (investigation board, full-screen only), dock (glass tool dock, no backdrop blur), frame3d (floating device frame for a screenshot, full-screen only) — + 8 trend hooks (pov, redact, island, search, glitch, flap, price, flip). Each docstring lists its fields and default zone; timing keys take phrases or seconds; screenshots (`src`, `before`/`after`) are files in `motion/clips`. Use them when `look.visuals.types` ticks them; they sit on the footage without blur/dim, never on the face (`"bg": true` = opaque full-screen B-roll page). After changing the library, run `.venv/bin/python tools/mograph.py --gallery work/<name>/decisions.json` and restart the Studio server so the Visuals → "✨ 2026 trends" tab loads the new catalog.
- **Natural colour:** iPhone HDR is converted with `tools/bin/tosdr` (Apple's tone mapping) before editing — uploads do it automatically. Off the Mac, ffmpeg's zscale tone mapping does it.

## Layout
- `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` holds the design system (layout, motion and editing rules from the original project; its colour and font tokens are overridden by the ReliefNow brand at the top of that file). Update it when the ReliefNow team changes the look.
- `./vedit` is the editor CLI (`tools/vedit.py`; `vedit.cmd` on Windows). Run `./vedit doctor` to check the setup, `./vedit studio` to start the Studio, and `./tools/setup.sh` (Mac) or `tools/setup.ps1` (Windows) to install.
- `input/` holds raw videos · `work/<name>/` holds per-video transcript, decisions and plans · `output/` holds previews, finals and .srt · `assets/` holds logo, music, SFX
